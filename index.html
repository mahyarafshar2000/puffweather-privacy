Puff Weather · Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Fiddle Leaf Studio

The short version: Puff Weather has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It only uses your location (if you allow it) to show the weather where you are. Your settings stay on your phone.

1. What Puff Weather uses

  • Your location (optional). If you tap "Use my location" and allow it, Puff reads your phone's location once to find your weather. You can say no and search for a city instead.
  • City searches. The city names you type are used to find matching places.
  • Your settings. Saved cities, °C or °F, alert choices and widget settings are stored only on your phone.

Puff Weather does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

2. Who receives data, and why

To show the weather, the app sends the minimum needed directly from your phone to these services:

  • Open-Meteo (weather data): the latitude and longitude of the place you're viewing, and the city names you search. See their terms and privacy.
  • BigDataCloud (place names): only when you use "Use my location", your coordinates are sent once to find the name of your city.

Like any internet service, these providers can see your phone's IP address when the app connects to them. We do not send them anything that identifies you personally, and we do not run our own servers.

3. Background updates, widgets and alerts

If you turn on weather alerts, lock-screen weather or add a home-screen widget, Puff checks the weather about every 30 minutes for the city you last chose in the app. It uses that saved city, not your live location. Puff does not track your location in the background.

Notifications are created on your phone. You can turn alerts and lock-screen weather off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

4. What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • We never sell or share your data.

5. Permissions

  • Location: only to show weather where you are, when you ask for it.
  • Notifications: only for weather alerts and lock-screen weather you turn on.
  • Internet: to download weather forecasts.

6. Deleting your data

Everything Puff stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it completely. We hold no copy.

7. Children

Puff Weather is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it uses data (for example, if ads are added in a future version), this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
